EXPERIMENT DESIGN

Chronology comes first.

Development ends in December 2024. Three outer walk-forward windows compare models. Each fit has two inner 63-session windows, six-session purges, and a separate chronological probability-calibration window.

Optuna explores 128 bounded candidates in total, with train-only feature selection. Ridge and LightGBM each compare pooled, per-stock and history-weighted hybrid predictions. Hyperparameters, fitted models and the selected candidate are frozen and hashed before the one-time 2025 test.

There is no claim of a globally optimal model. The test is now consumed; 2026 prices remain excluded.

An actionable target

At close t, predict the price return from open t+1 to open t+6. Enter at the next open plus adverse slippage. Exit after five sessions. No leverage, short selling or live broker orders.

Cash and risk

₹10 lakh starting capital. Maximum 90% entry exposure, 10% per stock and 30% per sector proxy. New buys use settled cash only under a conservative T+2 assumption. A 20% portfolio drawdown halts new entries. Minimum trailing daily turnover: ₹2 crore.

DATA & LIMITATIONS

Indian data. Honest limits.

Raw vendor responses and checksums are cached locally. OHLC is split-normalized; nominal historical prices are reconstructed. Dividends are non-spendable receivables on the ex-date. Non-cash corporate actions remain unreconciled.

The fixed universe contains currently surviving companies. It does not represent a point-in-time index-membership history. Sector indices are proxies. Daily data does not verify auction depth, circuit queues or partial fills.

Unperformed ablations

Point-in-time Indian news and fundamental vintages were unavailable. No current news is backfilled into history. ORB and VWAP require intraday data and were not approximated from daily candles.

What “net” includes

Brokerage, STT, stamp duty, exchange/SEBI/IPFT fees, GST, sell-side DP charges and adverse slippage. The current tariff is applied uniformly across history. Investor tax, data subscriptions and invoice rounding are excluded.

Bootstrap intervals are conditional on the selected strategy and observed year. They do not adjust for all model-search selection effects.
